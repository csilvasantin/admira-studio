# Coffee journey

In the Creador options, **Messages as the cup arrives** enables one editable line per panel, ordered from left to right. The existing headline appears first. Each local line enters as the centre of the cup crosses its panel's left edge, using the same global travel geometry as the exported photograph. The first panel starts immediately.

Spanish and English are separate full exports. Select both content languages to produce two complete programmes, rather than splitting the wall by language. A seven-panel wall with portrait and landscape individual endings exports 18 MP4 files: nine per language. At 24 seconds of travel and eight of closing, concatenate ES then EN for a 64-second shared-clock cycle.

**Animated steam following the cup** draws a periodic transparent sprite on the coffee surface. The canvas preview and native encoder share its size, anchor, frame count and frequency. Native exports include the effect inside the MP4, so player overlays are unnecessary. Individual photographs retain their original treatment. The original cup photograph should have its coffee surface centred at 50% width and 40.5% height; this effect is authored for that image, without automatic image analysis.

`journeyStory` is versioned as `admira.coffee-journey.v1`, validates both language arrays and excludes the previous three-act `wallStory`. Unsupported screen counts and durations produce planning errors. Imported configurations must preserve the real installation geometry and the separately verified physical player bindings; application names do not establish monitor position.

Validation: campaign journey tests cover timed message changes, independent language jobs, steam file/filter integration and invalid contracts. Native render and actual hardware playback require a separate check.

**Lower copy** provides a second editable line per panel and language, entering with its arrival message. `lowerCopy` is optional so earlier saved campaigns retain their general CTA. New stories initialise seven different lower lines; custom panel counts are supported. Imports reject missing language entries, empty lines and lines over 100 characters.
